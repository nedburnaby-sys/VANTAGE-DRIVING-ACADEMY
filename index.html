FAQ

Common questions

Do you offer lessons for Class 7 drivers?

Yes. Vantage offers driving instruction for Class 7 learners, including beginner skill development and road-test preparation.

Can I book lessons before a road test?

Yes. Road-test preparation can focus on consistency, observation, manoeuvres, traffic situations, and areas where you want extra practice.

Do you offer Class 5 driving lessons?

Yes. Class 5 lessons can help drivers refine their skills and prepare for the full-licence road test.

I already have driving experience. Can I take a refresher lesson?

Yes. Refresher training is useful if you have been away from driving or simply want more confidence and feedback.
